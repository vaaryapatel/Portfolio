About me

Hi, I'm Vaarya! I'm a Data Science student at Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management & Engineering in Mumbai, and I love finding the story hiding inside a messy dataset. I work with Python, machine learning and dashboards, and I've built a cricket performance predictor, a personal finance dashboard, and a placement-prep guide. I'm currently working on an AI-powered customer churn system and learning RAG and deep learning on the side. During my internship at Sony Pictures Networks India, I built an ad sales dashboard in SAP SAC, and I was told my communication and presentation skills stood out, which I'll happily take as a compliment.

Away from my laptop, life is a lot more delicious. I'm happiest with a good book in hand, or in the kitchen baking something that makes the whole house smell amazing, and then eating it, because good food is the best reward for hard work. I love exploring new places, and I'll shop whenever I get the chance (retail therapy is a valid coping strategy, and I have the receipts to prove it). I also volunteer with children in a slum community, teaching them concentration techniques and helping with their studies. It's the part of my week that teaches me the most.

I like to build things that are smart, useful and a little bit fun, just like my "11:11" playlist. Press play, make a wish, and keep scrolling. ✨
